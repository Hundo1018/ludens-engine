Audio assets of ludens-engine (assets/audio/)

calm_pad.lsound, calm_perc.lsound, tense_pad.lsound and music.music were synthesized by scripts/assets/gen_audio.py from
sums of sine partials, a decaying sine and a linear congruential noise. No recording, sample library or third-party
material is in them.
