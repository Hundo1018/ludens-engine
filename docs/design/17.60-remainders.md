ω3 / third-order cumulant, 3-D free surface, and 3-D particles (with per-cell Enright reseeding).
This note checks each against the source, states what exists, and decides whether the item closes
now or is deferred with its design written down. No code is changed here.

The "未做" line in the 17.60 entry says "3-D 自由表面" without qualification. The FLIP solver
above is a 3-D free surface in a closed box, so that line is too broad. The open part is narrower:
a 3-D free surface on the MAC grid (a level set or volume fraction), and open boundaries for any
free surface. The entry's own "現況" paragraph (no free surface, no grid projection) predates
`FlipWater3` and `MacFluid3`, and is stale on those two points. This note does not rewrite that
paragraph; the 17.60 progress note records the correction.

## 1. Third-order cumulant (ω3)

Scope: the third- and fourth-order cumulants of the D3Q27 collision (`Lbm27`). Today they are set
to zero after collision, which is the second-order cumulant scheme of Geier et al. 2015 with the
higher orders at rate 1. The cumulant scheme with ω3 relaxation needs:

- the third-order central moments as a separate relaxation target, with a rate that depends on
  the viscosity (or a free parameter), and
- a limiter or a stability check, since third-order relaxation is where D3Q27 cumulant schemes
  become unstable at low viscosity.

Test that would close it: a parity check that ω3 = 1 reproduces the current output bit for bit
(the golden of `test_lbm_cumulant` stays unchanged), and a decaying shear wave at two viscosities
where the third-order rate changes the damping. The damping must match the analytic rate within a
stated tolerance, and the run must stay finite for a stated number of steps at the low-viscosity
end.

Status: not implemented; scheduled as step 1 of the Decision.

## 2. 3-D free surface on the MAC grid

Scope: a level set (or volume fraction) on `MacFluid3`, so that the free surface of the MAC
solver is tracked the way `LevelSetWater2` tracks it in 2-D. The solver would need:

- a 3-D level set with reinitialisation. `LevelSetWater2` has a frequency (`reinit_every`, steps
  between reinitialisations) but no redistance iteration count, so the 3-D version has to define
  that parameter itself,
- the air-cell masking in the pressure solve, and
- open boundaries, or a closed box only (as `FlipWater3` already is).

Test that would close it: the 3-D dam break compared with the 2-D `LevelSetWater2` run at a
matching cross-section, and the closed-box rest state with zero velocity to a stated tolerance,
as `test_flip3` does for FLIP.

Status: not implemented; scheduled as step 2 of the Decision. `FlipWater3` covers the closed-box
free surface by particles; the MAC level set does not exist.

## 3. 3-D particles (per-cell Enright reseeding)

Scope: the particle level set in 3-D. `LevelSetParticles` (2-D) reseeds particles in a batch: the
ROADMAP entry lists per-cell Enright reseeding as not done (the batch replay was tried and is
recorded there). A 3-D version needs:

- per-cell reseeding of particles in 3-D voxels, and
- the matching 3-D level-set update.

The 2-D progress note records one negative result: a 9 % overshoot at the particle water line did
not move with seed bandwidth beyond 3 or with a halved time step. The source of that overshoot is
not located. A 3-D version would inherit it unless the cause is found first.

Status: not implemented; scheduled as step 3 of the Decision.

## Decision

The three items are ordinary numerical work, not GPU or UI, and none is blocked by the environment.
They stay open in the 17.60 entry until built, in this order:

1. ω3 (`Lbm27`): the third-order rate, with the parity gate (ω3 = 1 leaves `test_lbm_cumulant`
   bit-identical) and the two-viscosity shear-wave damping check.
2. 3-D MAC level set (`MacFluid3`): dam break against the 2-D `LevelSetWater2` cross-section, and a
   closed-box rest state, as `test_flip3` does against `FlipWater2`.
3. 3-D particles: after the 2-D +9 % overshoot source is located, since the 3-D reseeding would copy
   its structure.

The FLIP closed-box free surface (`FlipWater3`) is done and is not on this list.
