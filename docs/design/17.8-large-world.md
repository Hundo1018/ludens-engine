# 17.8 Large world coordinates

## Decision (2026-10-09)

f32 + origin rebasing + world partition first; f64 only as far as a `WorldType` that can be switched,
with small-world parity.

## Coordinates

The host speaks f64 world coordinates (`WorldPos`); the simulation runs in f32 local coordinates
`local = world - origin`, with `origin` an f64 kept by `Rebaser`. The conversion happens in f64, so a body
the host places 1e7 m out arrives in the scene with the same bits as one placed at the origin (tested:
a stack at (1e7, 0, -1e7) m runs bit-identically to the stack at the origin).

`CoordStrategy` decides when the origin moves:

| strategy | when | shift |
|---|---|---|
| `AbsoluteF32` | never | — (the engine before 17.8) |
| `RebasedF32(threshold, grid)` | observer beyond `threshold` m on any axis | observer position snapped to `grid` (power of two) |

Snapping to a power-of-two grid keeps the origin and every shift exactly representable, and makes `p - d`
exact for coordinates on that grid. Every shift is logged with its tick and saved with the scene.

## Shift

`ContactScene6.shift_origin(d)` moves every world-space quantity by `-d`: body positions (all slots), the
world-space manifold points in the warm-start and sensor caches, soft-body particles, and static triangle
meshes / heightfields, which store world coordinates — those are rebuilt through their constructors from
shifted source data, so BVH, normals and block bounds are what an `add` at the new coordinates would give.
Body-relative state (joint anchors, `ra` / `rb`, drive targets) and translation-invariant state
(velocities, impulses, islands, sleep) stay. The broadphase is rebuilt every step.

Measured: a resting stack shifted at tick 120 keeps all its contacts, gets no impulse (top-box speed stays
0) and after 60 more ticks differs from an unshifted run by 3.5e-6 m. Save after the shift, reload and
continue is bit-identical to continuing.

## Partition

`WorldPartition(cell_size, load_radius, unload_radius)` owns body specs in world coordinates. `update`
(between steps):

1. live dynamic bodies are re-owned by the cell they are in now;
2. wanted cells = cells within `load_radius` of an observer;
3. loaded cells beyond `unload_radius` of every observer unload: their bodies are captured (pose,
   velocity) and removed;
4. wanted cells not loaded load: their specs are spawned at `world - origin`;
5. a live body whose owner cell is not loaded is captured.

`unload_radius > load_radius` is the hysteresis: an observer pacing along a border causes no events after
the first visit (tested). Unload + reload restores pose and velocity bit-for-bit when the origin did not
move in between (the world ↔ local round trip is exact).

## f64

`WorldType` is a plain comptime alias. A conditional alias (`float64 if is_defined[...] else float32`) loses
the static floating-point evidence `cos` / `sin` / `acos` require, and `mojo precompile` takes no `-D`, so
the f64 variant is a build of a copy of the sources with that one line switched (`scripts/build_f64.sh`,
about a minute). Making it build took explicit conversions in 13 files: the GPU modules now use `WorldType`
/ `Real` instead of `Float32` (identical in the f32 build), `scheduler/rng.mojo` converts its f32 draws,
and the C-ABI shim keeps C `float` at the boundary with explicit conversions.

| distance | f32 absolute | f32 rebased | f64 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 m | 7.6e-6 | 7.6e-6 | 2.5e-14 |
| 1e4 m | 3.1e-2 | 1.2e-4 | 1.2e-10 |
| 1e5 m | 0.5 (does not move) | 1.2e-4 | 1.6e-9 |
| 1e7 m | 0.5 | 3.9e-7 | 3.0e-8 |

(|travel − 0.5 m| of a body moving 0.5 m/s for 1 s; `bench_large_world`.)

## Not here

`Runtime` / `EmbedWorld` / C-ABI integration of shift and partition; moving navigation and AI world data
with the origin; jointed bodies spanning cells; xz-only cells; box / sphere specs only.
